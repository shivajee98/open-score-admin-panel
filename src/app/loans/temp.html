Ancient Folklore

Long ago, nestled deep within the emerald valleys, a legend spoke of the hidden glades where the light dances upon dew-kissed leaves. It is said that those who carry a true heart may find the path when the morning mist rises.

The old texts whisper of prosperity brought forth not by gold, but by the quiet luck hidden in the earth. Guard these words, for they are woven from the memories of the soil and the old woods.
